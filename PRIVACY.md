# Privacy

Counter collects nothing.

- **No network.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so Android
  will not let it make any network connection. It contains no ads, analytics,
  crash reporting or tracking libraries.
- **What is stored, and where.** Your counters (name, colour, step, target,
  current count and rounds), a daily total per counter, and your settings are
  stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing else is recorded.
- **Backups.** If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may
  include this app's data in that backup, the same as for any other app. That is
  between you and your backup provider; the app itself sends nothing.
- **Deleting data.** Deleting a counter deletes its history. Uninstalling the app
  or clearing its storage deletes everything.
- **Permissions.** The app itself asks only for `VIBRATE`, for the click on each
  count and the buzz at a target. The widget library adds `WAKE_LOCK`,
  `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`,
  which Android uses to schedule widget updates. None of them lets the app
  connect to the internet or read your personal data.
